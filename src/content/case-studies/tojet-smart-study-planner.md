## Overview

TOJET was built to make studying easier, but content alone wasn’t enough. Students were juggling too many subjects, limited time, and no clear idea where to begin.

We designed a personalized study planner that worked like a digital academic advisor, generating a study path around each student’s goals, grade level, available time, and timeline.

![TOJET Smart Study Planner overview](/case-studies/tojet-smart-study-planner/overview.png "wide")

## The Problem

Students often came in overwhelmed by too many subjects, limited time, and no clear starting point.

The pattern was consistent: they studied randomly, lost momentum, and didn’t know how to build a plan. The problem wasn’t just access to lessons—they needed direction.

## Product Goal

The goal was to create a personalized study roadmap in minutes—reducing decision fatigue, supporting daily consistency, adapting to student progress, and working across multiple learning goals.

## Planner Inputs

The planner generated each roadmap from a small set of meaningful inputs: study goal, academic major, grade level, available daily study time, preferred duration, and selected subjects.

![TOJET planner input flow](/case-studies/tojet-smart-study-planner/planner-inputs.png "wide")

## Free Study

We also introduced a Free Study mode for students who wanted flexible access to all subjects without committing to a fixed schedule.

![TOJET Free Study mode](/case-studies/tojet-smart-study-planner/free-study.png "wide")

## Keeping Setup Lightweight

We reduced the planner setup from roughly 8–10 inputs to 4–5 focused steps, bringing plan creation down to around 2–3 minutes.

Because students were already under study pressure, onboarding needed to feel fast, calm, and manageable. This followed [Nielsen Norman Group’s progressive disclosure principle](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/progressive-disclosure/), revealing only what was necessary at each step instead of presenting every decision at once.

## Daily Plan Structure

Each day was structured around three clear actions: new lessons, timely reviews, and practice tests.

A clear next-action entry point helped students start immediately, reducing decision time from around 20–30 seconds to under 10 and keeping the focus on what needed to happen today.

![TOJET daily study plan structure](/case-studies/tojet-smart-study-planner/daily-plan-structure.png "wide")

## Smart Review Scheduling

After each lesson, a short quiz score shaped when that topic returned: stronger performance pushed reviews further out, while lower scores brought them back sooner.

This scheduling logic was informed by research on [spacing and retrieval practice](https://www.nature.com/articles/s44159-022-00089-1), which shows how distributing learning over time and actively retrieving information can strengthen learning and long-term retention.

The planner prioritized what needed attention most, while missed tasks rolled forward automatically so the schedule could adapt without breaking momentum.

![TOJET smart review scheduling](/case-studies/tojet-smart-study-planner/smart-review-scheduling.png "wide")

## Motivation Without Pressure

We wanted consistency without adding pressure.

The planner worked alongside TOJET’s motivation system—points, league progression, visible progress, and small wins—to make daily study feel rewarding rather than forced.

![TOJET motivation system](/case-studies/tojet-smart-study-planner/motivation-without-pressure.png "wide")

## Supporting Multiple Goals

Some students were working toward multiple goals at the same time, from school exams and entrance exam preparation to flexible Free Study.

The planner supported multiple active plans and easy switching between them, so students could manage different goals without losing structure.

![TOJET multiple study goals](/case-studies/tojet-smart-study-planner/supporting-multiple-goals.png "wide")

## Designed to Grow

The planning system was designed to scale, supporting more advanced logic and evolving user needs without changing the core experience.

## My Contribution

I led the product and UX design of the planner—from defining planning logic and user flows to building a scalable UI system in close collaboration with the founder.

## Outcomes

The final planner improved clarity, scalability, and study efficiency across the core experience.

![TOJET Smart Study Planner outcomes](/case-studies/tojet-smart-study-planner/outcomes.png "wide")

## What I Learned

Students don’t always need more content—often, they need more clarity.

A good planner doesn’t just organize tasks; it reduces friction, builds momentum, and helps people keep going.
