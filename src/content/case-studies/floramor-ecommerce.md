## Overview

Floramor is a premium Dubai flower shop focused on curated bouquets, gifting, and subscriptions.

I designed the end-to-end product ecosystem, from the customer storefront to an internal admin platform managing products, subscriptions, campaigns, and operations.

![Floramor e-commerce experience](/case-studies/floramor-ecommerce/overview.png "wide")

## Product Goal

To create a cohesive flower commerce experience that feels premium and effortless across discovery, purchase, and post-purchase journeys, while keeping complex operational logic behind the scenes.

The experience stays simple and intuitive for customers without sacrificing flexibility across different user needs and business constraints.

## My Role

End-to-end Product Design — UX Design · UI Design · User Flows · Design System · Admin Dashboard

## Discovering Floramor

The landing experience introduces Floramor’s collections, gifting, and subscriptions through a visually-led layout designed for discovery and conversion.

Key content includes featured collections, seasonal campaigns, subscription highlights, gifting content, and service information.

![Floramor landing experience](/case-studies/floramor-ecommerce/discovering-floramor.png "wide")

## Browsing the Shop

The shop was designed to make large product collections easy to explore through clear categories, collections, featured products, and flexible filtering and sorting.

![Floramor shop browsing experience](/case-studies/floramor-ecommerce/browsing-the-shop.png "wide")

## Product Details & Customization

The product page balances strong visual presentation with clear customization and purchase decisions, including product options, bouquet personalization, delivery details, related products, and a straightforward add-to-cart flow.

![Floramor product details and customization](/case-studies/floramor-ecommerce/product-details.png "wide")

## Cart & Checkout

The checkout flow keeps a complex delivery and payment process feeling fast and frictionless, guiding users from cart review and delivery details through address selection, payment method and gateway, and final order confirmation.

![Floramor cart and checkout flow](/case-studies/floramor-ecommerce/cart-checkout.png "wide")

## Blog

The blog provides inspiration and content around flowers, gifting, and floral care.

![Floramor blog and gifting content](/case-studies/floramor-ecommerce/blog.png "wide")

## Flower Subscriptions

Subscriptions became one of the most product-heavy parts of Floramor, supporting Home and Office plans across different bouquet sizes, flower selections, and presentation tiers.

The flow simplifies complex scheduling and billing through weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly deliveries, flexible plan duration, first-delivery scheduling, pay-per-delivery or prepaid billing, and optional add-ons—without making long-term flower delivery feel overwhelming.

![Floramor Home and Office subscription plans](/case-studies/floramor-ecommerce/flower-subscriptions.png "wide")

## Account & Subscription Management

A centralized profile gave users control over orders, subscriptions, invoices, saved addresses, and gifting reminders, including the ability to track order status and pause or cancel active plans.

![Floramor account and subscription management](/case-studies/floramor-ecommerce/account-management.png "wide")

## Managing the Floramor Ecosystem

Alongside the storefront, I designed a full admin platform for managing products, orders, customers, subscriptions, campaigns, users, and day-to-day operations.

The system supported product and category management, order and delivery tracking, subscription pricing and billing, promotions and promo codes, admin permissions, and operational reporting.

![Floramor admin platform](/case-studies/floramor-ecommerce/admin-platform.png "wide")

## Outcome

The final product brings together a premium storefront with a robust operational system powering subscriptions, deliveries, and customer management behind the scenes.
